# Gameplay bug audit

Source-level findings from the gameplay audit. The repository is an ILSpy extract without the original game dependencies, so findings were traced through code paths but not runtime-tested.

| # | Severity | Bug | Normal gameplay trigger and impact | Location |
|---|---|---|---|---|


| 1 | Medium | Rejected Wolf Pup transformations still grant rewards | During daytime Eclipse, a Wolf Pup repeatedly presses the transformation action. The prefix refuses transformation, but the unconditional postfix still restores hunger, grants Spirit Resistance and awards nearby Scientist research. Since transformation was rejected, the once-per-night flag does not stop repetition. | [TransformPatch.cs:50](LycansNewRoles/TransformPatch.cs#L50), [TransformClass.cs:157](LycansNewRoles/TransformClass.cs#L157) |
| 2 | Medium | Summoned spirits can act on stale targets at arbitrary distance | Aim at a nearby player, then keep aiming at a nearby door or wall while that player moves or teleports away. Scenery hits retain the previous target. Interacting still sends that target, and execution does not revalidate range or line of sight. Ghost healing, Specter curses and Cultist captures can therefore affect distant players. | [PlayerSummonedSpiritComponent.cs:426](LycansNewRoles/PlayerSummonedSpiritComponent.cs#L426), [PlayerCustom.cs:14585](LycansNewRoles/PlayerCustom.cs#L14585) |
| 3 | Medium | A disconnect during abduction blocks future Kidnapper casts | The victim disconnects during the three-second cast. Completion clears the timer, then dereferences the missing target. The catch only logs the exception, leaving the target reference populated. New abductions require that reference to be empty. | [PlayerCustom.cs:10750](LycansNewRoles/PlayerCustom.cs#L10750), [InteractPatch.cs:223](LycansNewRoles/InteractPatch.cs#L223) |
| 4 | Medium | A kidnapped Mercenary can still win its hunt | Abduct an already-hunting Mercenary during daylight and keep it confined until dusk. Abduction clears its effects but preserves the hunt flag. The dusk victory check requires it to be alive, but does not check kidnapping. | [GiveNewRolesPatch.cs:1088](LycansNewRoles/GiveNewRolesPatch.cs#L1088) |
| 5 | Medium | A kidnapped Cultist can start a playable hunt and win | Abduct a fully charged Cultist before the meeting. Hunt activation neither excludes nor releases kidnapped players. Its summoned spirit receives input focus, and capture/victory checks allow it to complete the hunt despite confinement. | [GiveNewRolesPatch.cs:1262](LycansNewRoles/GiveNewRolesPatch.cs#L1262), [CultistManager.cs:265](LycansNewRoles/CultistManager.cs#L265) |
| 6 | Medium | Draft can assign disabled secondary roles | Disable Sprinter and draft secondary roles. Multiplicity entries are added back after the enabled-role filter, reintroducing disabled Sprinter entries. Later compatibility checks do not check whether the role is enabled. | [DraftManager.cs:184](LycansNewRoles/DraftManager.cs#L184) |
| 7 | Medium | Scientist kill research uses the victim's diminishing returns | A Scientist with accumulated research witnesses another wolf kill. The award uses the victim's half-day progress instead of the Scientist's—usually zero—bypassing the Scientist's intended diminishing returns. Passive and transformation research correctly use the Scientist's progress. | [RpcKillPatch.cs:245](LycansNewRoles/RpcKillPatch.cs#L245) |
| 8 | Medium | Scientist can spend its gadget ability without receiving anything | Disable Smoke, Grenade, Sleeping Gas and Phasing Diamond while leaving another gadget enabled. Selection checks the broader enabled-gadget pool, then calls `.First()` on the empty Scientist-compatible subset. The exception is caught, but the caller still consumes the charge and starts the 60-second cooldown. | [PlayerCustom.cs:7278](LycansNewRoles/PlayerCustom.cs#L7278), [ability consumption:9840](LycansNewRoles/PlayerCustom.cs#L9840) |
| 9 | Medium | Host Paranoia prevents a remote Agent's assassination | An unaffected remote Agent attacks its partner at night while the host—or the host's observed player—has Paranoia. The acting client offers the interaction correctly, but server execution checks the server-local POV's Paranoia instead of the attacking Agent's. The valid action silently fails. | [PlayerCustom.cs:10150](LycansNewRoles/PlayerCustom.cs#L10150) |
| 10 | Medium | Detective intel can falsely identify the two Lovers as opposing camps | “Different sides” intel selects the wolf-side Lover first. That player enters the wolf-team branch, which leaves the villager-side Lover eligible as its supposed opposing-camp partner. Selecting the villager-side Lover first correctly excludes the partner. Lovers share their victory condition, so the resulting intel is false. | [PlayerCustom.cs:7801](LycansNewRoles/PlayerCustom.cs#L7801) |
